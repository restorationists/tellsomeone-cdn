It's Safe, Easy, and Free

Our 7-step process is designed to protect you whilst ensuring your testimony reaches authorities and safeguarders securely.

  1. 1. You Send

    Your report is sent through GlobalLeaks (trusted by the International Criminal Court), encrypted during transmission, and stored on secure servers protected by US First Amendment law.

  2. 2. Computer Review

    Our private AI system reviews submissions to identify your age and vulnerability, classify how severe it is, and whether it's an emergency. This protects human reviewers from trauma whilst ensuring nothing critical is missed.

  3. 3. Emergency Triage

    If you're in immediate danger, as an urgent case it could be immediately referred to trusted safeguarding charities who can provide professional support whilst maintaining your anonymity where possible under the law.

  4. 4. Legal Review

    Qualified barristers with expertise in criminal law review your report to identify potential crimes requiring police referral, ensuring compliance with legal obligations whilst protecting your rights.

  5. 5. Inquiry Access

    If you've allowed us, what you wrote can be securely transferred to the inquiry teams or safeguarding case workers under parliamentary privilege protection, ensuring your testimony can be used without fear of legal retaliation.

  6. 6. Redacted Sharing

    Only with your explicit consent, heavily redacted and anonymised versions may be shared with partner organisations or researchers. All identifying information is removed.

  7. 7. Protected Always

    Your identity and personal details remain permanently protected. We will never share your information without explicit consent unless you're in immediate physical danger.
