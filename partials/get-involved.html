You Can Do Something

Feeling helpless isn't an option. There are lawful, effective ways to help expose the truth and support victims. You can make a real difference.

Lawfully Collect Evidence and Intelligence

You don't need to be a detective to help gather crucial information to help the police in their duties. Learn proper techniques for documenting evidence securely, understand free open-source intelligence (OSINT) software tools, and support neighbourhood watch programs focused on child protection.

From helping someone make a report to learning professional investigation software, there are legitimate ways to contribute to uncovering the truth whilst staying within the law.

Demand Your Local MP Joins the Inquiry

Even the worst politicians respond to pressure from constituents. Your MP needs to hear why an inquiry matters and they must publicly support it. You can make it clear if any of their party were involved, they will face the wrath of the electorate.

Writing to your MP is one of the most powerful tools we have, even if it seems pointless at times. Use it to demand they take a public stance supporting the independent inquiry into rape gangs.

Write to Your MP

FOIA Your Local Council and Police Force

Freedom of Information Act requests are a powerful tool for transparency. Request documents, meeting minutes, policies, and correspondence related to child protection, grooming gang investigations, and institutional responses.

Every FOIA request creates a public record and forces institutions to account for their actions. The cumulative effect of hundreds of requests across the country will reveal patterns of negligence and cover-ups.

Make A FOIA Request

Donate Money To Reliable Charities

Victims need ongoing support, and proper charities need funding to provide it. Your donations help ensure survivors have access to counselling, legal support, and advocacy services. They are fighting an uphill battle against a major source of their own funding.

We've researched and vetted organisations who genuinely help victims rather than perpetuating the system which failed them. Your money can go to groups who prioritise victims over institutional reputation management.

Volunteer Your Time to Support Organisations

Charities and support organisations desperately need volunteers with widely-varying skills. Whether you can offer administrative support, professional expertise, or simply time to help with events and outreach, your contribution matters.

From supporting helplines to helping with fundraising events, volunteering connects you directly with efforts to help victims and prevent future abuse. Find opportunities that match your skills and availability.

Educate Your Own Networks on the Problem

Many people remain unaware of the true scope of the rape gang scandal or how cultural attitudes enable abuse. Giving talks at churches, clubs, community groups, and schools helps break through media silence and institutional denial.

Learn how to explain where "honour rape" culture comes from, how grooming operates, and what ordinary people can do to protect children. Education is the first step towards prevention.

Don't Take Matters Into Your Own Hands

However frustrated you feel, vigilante action undermines legitimate investigations and puts victims at greater risk. Even when police officers are involved in cover-ups, the solution is transparency and accountability, not taking the law into your own hands.

Support honourable constables and detectives by providing information through proper channels. The system needs reform, not replacement. Understanding the law helps you work within it effectively whilst demanding the changes necessary for justice.
