The website and all services are available 24/7 at no charge for any good faith British political organisation, charity, or concerned group to collect evidence of ongoing sexual crime from the documented, epidemic, and devastating rape gang activity in our country. We wrote to multiple organisations about their own attempts at offering "whistleblowing," services, outlining serious safeguarding concerns. We offered them sophisticated, military-grade technology for free, and every one of them simply ignored it.

We will never charge for it, never solicit donations, nor use the horror of this ongoing tragedy to promote any sectarian, partisan, or private cause. If you care about children, you can use this service to help them tell someone what happened to them.
