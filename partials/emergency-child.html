You Need Help Right Now

If someone is hurting you or about to hurt you, here's what to do:

Run

Get away from danger. Go to a shop, church, school, or anywhere with people. Lock doors. Stay in public places.

Trust your instincts. If it feels dangerous, it is.

Call for Help

999 - Police, Fire, Ambulance

If you're in immediate danger

0800 1111 - Childline

Free, confidential help

You can call from any phone, even without credit.

Find a Trusted Adult

Look for: teachers, police staff, a priest, neighbours you know, relatives who are safe.

Say: "I need help. Someone is hurting me and I'm not safe."

If you can't find anyone safe, stay in public places and keep calling the numbers above.
