Before You Start

  • Write down important details somewhere safe first
  • Find a quiet place where no one will bother you
  • Maybe use a computer at school or a friend's house

Keep Safe

  • Don't use a device the person who hurt you can see
  • Clear your internet history afterwards if needed
  • Have a story ready if someone asks what you're doing

Remember: You're not alone, this isn't your fault, and you're doing the right thing.
