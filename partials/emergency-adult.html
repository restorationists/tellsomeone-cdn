You're in Immediate Danger

If you've been attacked or are being threatened, take these steps now:

Get Somewhere Safe

Get to a safe location with other people. Lock doors. Stay in public view. Trust your instincts about danger.

Hospitals, police stations, shops, churches, friends' homes

Contact Emergency Services

999 - Emergency Services

Police, Fire, Ambulance

0808 500 2222 - Rape Crisis

Free, confidential help 24/7

Be clear: "I am in immediate danger and I need help right now."

Document Everything

Take photos of injuries, threats, or evidence. Save messages. Write down what happened with dates and times.

Store evidence safely: cloud storage, trusted friends, multiple copies
